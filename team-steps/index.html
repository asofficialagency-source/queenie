Diaspora Connect Global · DiaspoPlug

START HERE

Diaspora Connect Global (DCG) is the company. It connects diaspora communities with people, projects and partners they can trust.

DiaspoPlug is the app made by DCG. In the app, people connect, find chances and join circles.

The goal: every DCG account looks the same and clear. The company comes first. The profile photo is always the DCG logo.

How to use this page:

1. Pick a section. Open a step and do it.

2. Tap Copy to copy a text. Then paste it in the app.

3. Tick the box when the step is done. Your ticks stay saved on this device.

Who approves: Queenie checks every text, photo and banner before it goes live. Not sure? Ask her first.

Choices have up to 3 options. A is our pick.

10.4

Keep a weekly rhythm

3 to 4 posts a week.

›

Why: A steady rhythm keeps people coming back.

EACH WEEK
  1. 1 post about the app: one feature at a time.
  2. 1 post behind the scenes: the team, the meetings, the building.
  3. 1 post with a chance or good news for the diaspora.
  4. Optional: 1 post with Queenie, as a collab.

No politics, no bad news about countries, no promises about features that are not live yet.

Done when: You post 3 to 4 times every week.
