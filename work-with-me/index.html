About Queenie

Born in Brussels. Rooted in Ghana.

Both my parents are entrepreneurs, so I learned early what it takes to build something from nothing. Today I work between Europe and Africa every week.

People come to me when they want to build, hire, sell, ship or launch something across borders, but don't know the right people or how to move forward. I make the introductions, set up the structure and keep the work moving.

Questions

Before you book

Who do you work with?

Entrepreneurs, companies and investors who want to build, grow or invest between Europe and Africa. Some already run a business. Others still have a 9-to-5 and want to start. If you want to build across borders with structure, we can talk.

Is this only about Africa?

No. I work across borders, in Europe and in Africa. My network is strongest in Ghana and between Europe and Africa, so that is where I can help you most.

Do I need to live in Africa or travel all the time?

No. You can stay where you are. We set up the structure and the people on the ground, so the work moves without you being there every week.

Why does the intro call cost €50?

It keeps my calendar for people who are serious. I prepare with your answers, you get my full attention for 30 minutes, and you leave with a clear next step.

What does working together cost?

It depends on your project, the scope and the timeline. If we're a fit, I explain it on the call and you get a proposal in writing.

Can you promise me a deal or results?

No. An introduction is an open door, not a signed deal. We agree on clear goals and milestones, and we check them together.

How do you check the people you introduce?

I work with people I have tested myself. If I don't know the right person, I look in my network, and I tell you honestly when I can't find a match.

How long does a project take?

It depends on your sector and where you start. I prefer to work together for at least six months, with a review every three months. Good structure takes time.

Can you fund my project?

No, I don't fund projects. I help you with people, structure and direction.

Can I book you to speak or train my team?

Yes. I speak and train at conferences, universities, schools, companies and churches. Book an intro call and tell me it's for speaking or training.

I'm looking for a job or opportunities. Where do I go?

This page is for people who want to build or invest. For jobs and new contacts, join DiaspoPlug, our community app.
